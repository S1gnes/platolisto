# PlatoListo

PlatoListo tells students what they can cook from what is already in their fridge, and what to buy cheaply to finish a dish with this week's supermarket deals.

It has two ways in and one destination:

- **What I have.** Take a photo of the fridge. AI spots the ingredients, you fix the list, and dishes are ranked by how much you already have.
- **What's cheap now.** Start from this week's deals and get dishes built around discounted products.

Both lead to a recipe that shows what you have, what is missing, and where each missing item is on sale. Missing items go to a shopping list grouped by store.

## Who it is for

Students and young people who live on their own and cook on a budget, including exchange students. The launch market is Spain (Mercadona, Lidl, Carrefour), in English and Spanish.

## Business model

- **Sponsored deals.** Chains and brands pay to place their discounted products in recommended dishes. Sponsored deals are always labelled.
- **PlatoListo Plus.** The core app is free. The AI features (AI chef, bartender mode for 18+, weekly plan) are in Plus.
- No commission on purchases.

## About this repository

This is a working demo for a university Business class presentation. Supermarket prices are example data, and the Plus purchase is simulated.
